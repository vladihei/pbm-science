## What this test establishes

- The five largest providers in the supplied exports are ClinicalTrials.gov (983 IDs), CTRI (407), ReBEC (343), IRCT (198), and ChiCTR (95). Together they account for 2,026 of 2,235 distinct TrialIDs (90.6%). This is a measured priority list, not evidence that direct searches of those providers are complete. The exports contain 14 represented providers; WHO's provider list has 20, and six had no hits in these exports.
- RBR-10zzmvxf is a confirmed PBM record absent from the files but already recruitment-complete. NCT07271927 is only an unresolved indexed candidate; its record has not been verified and it must not be counted as an added trial.
- Direct source checking can clarify status. ReBEC explicitly reports completed recruitment, while an ICTRP “Not Recruiting” export value does not distinguish active-but-closed enrollment from completed or otherwise inactive trials.
- A broad monthly census is not yet demonstrated. This pass did not complete full direct queries or bulk exports for any of the five largest sources. ClinicalTrials.gov has an official API v2; ReBEC exposes an advanced-search table and individual ICTRP-format downloads; the CTRI, IRCT, and ChiCTR tests did not verify a convenient full-result export. Monthly effort and additional yield must be measured before describing the process as complete.
- Contact fields are not needed for the public directory. Keep raw exports private and omit personal contact data from every published snapshot.

## Rights and attribution checkpoint

The supplied snapshot comes from WHO ICTRP. Its terms require source attribution, current data, display of the date processed by ICTRP, no claim of ownership, and prohibit marketing, promotional, or commercial use. The supplied files did not contain the ICTRP processing date; provider import dates are not being represented as that date. Keep the missing date visible and resolve how the recurring source will meet the terms before describing the dataset as fully rights-cleared. A password is access control; it does not replace source-specific reuse terms.

For direct records, assess each registry's own reuse terms before reproducing its metadata. ISRCTN gives explicit reuse terms; ANZCTR's terms impose conditions on automated collection and use. ReBEC and ChiCTR do not yet have a verified bulk reuse license in this workflow. Record “not verified” as unresolved rather than treating absence of a discovered license as either permission or prohibition.
